Today: an entry's life on the Desk

  • Days 1 to 7The card shows on the Desk by itself. "Just today" fades the older ones back.
  • After 7 daysThe card leaves the Desk on its own. The entry and its hours stay on Day, the Timesheet, Items and Find.
  • If you moved itIt stays on the Desk for good. Nothing takes it off except filing it in a drawer or deleting it.
  • DeleteRemoves the hours too, so it's the wrong tool for tidying.

What's missing

A way to say "I'm done looking at this" without losing the hours. Notes already have Archive; entries don't.

Nothing is ever thrown away on its own. Entries only go off the Desk; they never disappear from your records.
